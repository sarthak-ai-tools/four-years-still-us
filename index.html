Kukii.. it's been 4 years already and I still wonder how can someone just walk into my life in the most unexpected way and become the cutest, the prettiest, the hottest, the sweetest, the bestest, the most soulful, the most charming, the most important person of my life. Dreamy FR! (nahin uthna ab mujhe iss sapne se)

Little did I know I will long to listen to the most pointless things about your day, I will love to celebrate you everyday... itni achchhi aadat kaise lag gayi kya pata.. Itne zyaada pasandida bann gaye ho ki mujhe ab mai hi pasand nahin aata...meri Kaju Katli ne kuch toh jaadu kar diya hai (kabhi matt rokna 🤭).

Mujhe kabhi nahin laga tha meet pe koi half date ke liye poochega and within a month Bombayy meri haqeeqat bann jaayegi.. couldn't ask for anything better.

Na jaane kaise isi beech mein kahin, aapki kahaani, aapke sapne, aapki pasand napasand, sab mere apne bann gaye.. Na jaane kaise mere nazron ki aadat bann gaye aap, mera favourite contact, mera pinned chat, meri lifeline, meri subah ki shuruaat, raat ka sukoon, mera intezaar, meri hasi.. sab kuch..

Mai bekhabar tha ki har moment pe kisiki itni yaad bhi aa sakti hai, until it was me & my 'Kaash meri Kukii yahaan hoti..' (poore din mere khyaalon mein chalke thakte nahin kya madam? 😉)

And all these became possible only & only because of you my dearest.. the way you put in all the efforts, distance bas ek word lagta hai ab... you're there in my every wins, every hardships.. meri strength bannke, and I am super super grateful for you meri Princess ❤️

itna kuch kehna hai.. but literally going speechless while typing these.. Fattu being Fattu ofc! ankahi baatein aap samajh jaana..

You made me believe, there is a space that is so comfortable that I can speak my heart out, without any hesitation.. express myself unfiltered.. thank you for being my safe space meri Rasmalaiiii!! (woh 10/10 rasmalai wali photo.. 11/10 thii..)

Pata hai mujhe sabseeee zyaada kya pasand hai... your presence, the way you talk.. the way you forget everything & zone out when you’re with me.. Kukii being Kukii is my fav genre, my little cute kid.

And ofcourse when you send random snaps of yourself enjoying, living the moment, smiling with those sparkling eyes.. I miss those post-lunch cuteee snaps..

I know how much you love being loved, and someone having a heart like yours deserves it all.. the entire ocean & beyond.. You'll never have to seek for it, I promise 🤞, love will always come to you naturally, in the most divine ways..

aap kabhi kuch nahin maangte merese, and this really motivates ki poori duniyaa meri Devi ji ke kadmon mein laa doon..

Dekho kitnii pyaari smileee aa gayiii meri laado ko 🥰

Yaar kukii.. how can someone be friend with someone so boring, so uncool, so silent and so cold like me... I don't even deserve the tiniest bit of yours honestly..

umm.. bas ek complaint hai kukii se..

there are moments you go silent, you become reserved.. and all I can do is just notice everything, helplessly. I want to build that comfort zone for you.. I really want to 🫴

I don't want to overstep to make you feel weak, but jaise aap meri taakat ho, I want to reciprocate the same.. how can I let you deal with all these things alone, I can't..

My world loses it's colour jab aap udaas ho jaate ho.. bechain ho jaata hoon ki aisa kya kardu bas mai..

Aap kaafi baar kuch baatein karna chahte ho, and then woh baatein hoti hi nahin kabhi... and I might not show it evidently but I think about those a lot..

i miss our movie nights, i miss our video calls, i miss receiving random photos, i miss knowing about your day, i miss listening to my person yap... i miss making silly excuses when you ask "Neend nahin aati na jab tak mai ghar na aa jau?" nahin aati na madam.. accepted.

But everything is fine, jab tak meri madam is happy happy, bas DHYAAN RAKHA KARO apna.. and needless to say.. mai yahin hoon, humesha! ♾️
